# Design Context — Scout

## Target audience
One operator: a fast, end-to-end software builder who can also sell. He uses Scout to get hired at
startups through the side door (show up with a working demo + a sharp founder DM, not an application).
Technical, impatient, has taste, hunts for an edge. Not a team, not enterprise. A power user.

## Use cases (jobs to be done)
1. Scan a ranked list of target startups and instantly see which one he has the most chance at.
2. Open one company's "brief": its real problems (with evidence), the one thing to build (with a time
   estimate and whether an OSS project exists to glue), and a ready-to-send founder DM.
3. Work the top targets one at a time; track outcome (shortlisted, dm sent, replied, call booked, dead)
   so the scoring calibrates over time.
4. Hit "start this build" to scaffold a POC repo, and "refresh DM" before sending.

## Brand contract
The locked visual contract lives in DESIGN.md at the project root (Vercel x Pitchfork remix,
"The Startup Review", 2026-06-11). DESIGN.md wins over anything below and over any tool's house
style. The sections above (audience, jobs) still apply.

## Hard rules
- No emojis. No em dashes in copy (periods, commas, parentheses).
- 60fps; respect prefers-reduced-motion with a beautiful static fallback.
- Avoid AI-slop tells: no glow-on-dark, no purple/blue gradients, no gradient text on numbers,
  no glassmorphism, no animated status dots, no identical card grids.
